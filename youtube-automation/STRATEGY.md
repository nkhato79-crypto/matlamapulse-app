# 30-Day Faceless YouTube Monetization Strategy

## Target: 1,000 Subscribers + 4,000 Watch Hours in 30 Days

### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-3)
- Set up channel: name, banner, profile picture, description
- Upload 5-10 long-form videos (8-12 min each) immediately
- Upload 10-15 Shorts simultaneously
- Optimize all SEO: titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails

### Phase 2: Volume Attack (Days 4-14)
- **Daily output: 2-3 long videos + 3-5 Shorts**
- Post Shorts at peak times: 12pm, 5pm, 8pm EST
- Post long-form at: 2pm or 6pm EST
- Cross-post Shorts to TikTok and Instagram Reels
- Reply to EVERY comment within 1 hour

### Phase 3: Optimization (Days 15-21)
- Analyze which videos get most views/retention
- Double down on winning topics and formats
- A/B test thumbnails (change underperforming ones)
- Start pinning top comments with CTA
- Create playlists to boost watch time

### Phase 4: Push to Monetization (Days 22-30)
- Focus on Shorts for subscriber growth (viral potential)
- Focus on 15-20 min videos for watch hours
- Engage in niche communities (Reddit, Twitter, Discord)
- Collaborate with similar-sized channels

## Content Formula

### Long-Form Structure (8-12 min)
1. **Hook** (0-5 sec): Bold claim or shocking stat
2. **Problem** (5-30 sec): Why viewer should care
3. **Content** (30 sec - 10 min): Deliver value with pattern interrupts
4. **CTA** (last 30 sec): Subscribe + comment question

### Shorts Structure (30-60 sec)
1. **Hook** (0-2 sec): Immediate grab
2. **One insight** (2-50 sec): Single actionable tip
3. **Loop/CTA** (last 5 sec): "Follow for part 2" or seamless loop

## SEO Checklist
- [ ] Title: Under 60 chars, curiosity gap, power words
- [ ] Description: Main keyword in first sentence, 200 words
- [ ] Tags: 15-20 tags, mix broad + specific
- [ ] Thumbnail: High contrast, 3-4 words max, readable at small size
- [ ] End screen: Subscribe button + next video
- [ ] Cards: Link to playlist at pattern interrupt moments
